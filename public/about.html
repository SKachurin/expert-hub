ExpertHub

Expert marketplace inside Telegram

Expert Hub is a Telegram-native marketplace where experts sell consultations, clients book available slots, and payments run through TON smart contracts.

Why it matters

One product, three clear benefits.

For clients
Easy access to trusted experts
Book consultations faster and see reviews linked to real paid sessions.
For experts
Turn Telegram audience into bookings
Create a public page, connect calendar, set price, and receive prepaid bookings.
For Telegram + TON
A new use case inside the ecosystem
Expands everyday usage for TON + Adds useful service for Telegram platform.

What problem it solves

Today the expert business flow inside Telegram is mostly manual.

Experts waste time in private chats
They manually answer requests, negotiate time, explain pricing, and ask for prepayment.
Clients struggle to verify reputation
Reviews on the internet are easy to fake or buy, so trust is hard to measure.
Booking, payment, and trust are disconnected
The full flow usually lives across separate tools with no common proof.

How Expert Hub works

A paid consultation flow inside Telegram, secured by TON smart contract logic.

1
Expert creates a public page
2
Connects calendar and TON wallet
3
Client picks a free slot
4
Client prepays through TON smart contract
5
Expert confirms the booking slot
6
Bot creates a conference call at the confirmed time and invites customer and expert
7
If the expert connects to the call, money is released to the expert
8
Client can write a review
9
Review is linked to the real paid consultation

Why the trust model is stronger

Reviews are not just text left anywhere on the internet.

Reviews are connected to paid bookings
That makes reputation more transparent and harder to fake.
Clients see a clearer signal of real expertise
It becomes easier to distinguish real demand from paid promotion or reputation inflation.
TON is used for a practical everyday action
Actual booking and payment infrastructure for expert services.
